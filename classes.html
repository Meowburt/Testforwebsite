FAQ

Good to know

How does a mixology class work?

We'll bring everything but the alcohols of choice needed for the experience, including bar tools, mixers, garnishes, glassware, and instruction. Our mixologist will guide your group through each cocktail step-by-step while keeping the atmosphere fun and beginner-friendly!

Do we need any bartending experience?

Not at all! Our classes are designed for all skill levels. Whether you're a cocktail enthusiast or have never held a shaker before, we'll walk you through every step.

Can we choose the cocktails we'll make?

Absolutely! One of the best parts of the experience is customizing the menu. We'll work with you to select cocktails that fit your group's preferences, event theme, or favorite flavors.

What types of events are mixology classes great for?

Our classes are perfect for bachelorette parties, birthdays, girls' nights, team-building events, bridal showers, holiday parties, and any gathering looking for a unique interactive experience.

How many people can attend a class?

We can accommodate both intimate groups and larger celebrations. During your consultation, we'll discuss your guest count and create an experience that works best for your group.

Check your Date!

Give us the details, we'll handle the drinks. Share your wedding date, venue, and guest count, and we'll create a bar experience that's perfectly suited to your celebration.
